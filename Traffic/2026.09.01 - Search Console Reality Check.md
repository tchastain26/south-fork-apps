# South Fork Apps — Search Console Reality Check

Source: Google Search Console export, `southforkapps.com`, last 3 months (2026.06.03 to 2026.09.01).
Raw export: `00_INBOX/southforkapps.com-Performance-on-Search-2026-09-01.zip`

## The headline

| Metric | 90 days |
|---|---|
| Clicks | **28** |
| Impressions | 9,076 |
| CTR | 0.31% |
| Average position | ~54 |

That is **0.3 clicks per day** from Google. Roughly 9 visitors a month.

## Why AdSense on this site earns nothing

The 2026.05.02 log recorded "670 unique visitors in 24 hours" after the mega-build.
That was a launch spike, not a baseline. The baseline is 9 search visitors a month.

At the rate in the Compiled News decision memo (about $20 per 10,000-20,000 pageviews),
search traffic at this level earns **cents per month, not dollars**. Turning on ad
coverage across 269 pages was correct and cost nothing, but the input is near zero.
Ads are not a broken mechanism here. There is no traffic to monetize.

## Where the pages actually rank

| Average position | Pages | Impressions |
|---|---|---|
| 1-10 | 15 | 393 |
| 11-20 | 13 | 437 |
| 21-40 | 60 | 1,415 |
| 41-60 | 101 | 3,370 |
| 61+ | 67 | 4,148 |

**83% of impressions come from pages at position 41 or worse**, which is page 5 and
beyond. Those impressions can never become clicks. No title or metadata change moves
a page from 54 to the first page.

## The one real bright spot: music tools

Music apps are the only cluster that ranks:

| Page | Position | Impressions | Clicks | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| lil-beat-maker | **5.5** | 310 | 2 | 0.65% |
| symbol-pad | 12.8 | 81 | 3 | 3.7% |
| rhythm-challenge | 19.8 | 36 | 2 | 5.6% |
| chromatic-scale | 19.1 | 13 | 0 | 0% |
| metronome | 19.8 | 10 | 0 | 0% |

`lil-beat-maker` ranks position 5 but converts at 0.65%. Position 5 should return
roughly 5-8%. That gap is a title and snippet problem, and it is the only page on the
site where fixing the title is worth doing. Query "music theory cheat sheet" also
returned a click. The 36 music apps are the site's only defensible niche.

## Confirmed dead ends

- **Head terms.** "agenda builder" (229 impressions, position 79), "loan payment
  calculator" (150, position 75), "meeting agenda builder" (58, position 86). The site
  is indexed for these and buried. It will not out-rank entrenched competitors.
- **Sitewide title optimization.** Pointless below position ~20. Applies to 228 of 256 pages.
- **FAQ schema.** Google restricted FAQ rich results to government and health sites in 2023.

## What this means

Do not spend more time trying to turn this site into a traffic business. The route to
money from South Fork Apps is the productized build service
(`detailing-quote-widget`, $49, PayPal checkout already live), and that needs
distribution to service-business owners, which is a different channel from this
site's organic search entirely.
