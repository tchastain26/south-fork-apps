Fictional demonstration only. These are sample orders, not South Fork income or customer results. No uploads or tracking. The sample script runs locally with Python's standard library.

Agreed rules

  1. Trim order IDs; match duplicates by order ID.
  2. Keep the first exact duplicate. Stop on a conflicting duplicate.
  3. Use integer cents; calculate net as gross minus refund. Stop on missing IDs, invalid amounts or refunds above gross.

The raw sample has four rows, including one exact duplicate. The result has three unique orders.
