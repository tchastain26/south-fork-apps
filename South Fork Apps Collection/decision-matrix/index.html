Decision Matrix

Compare multiple options against weighted criteria and let the math show which choice actually wins. Add your own criteria, assign importance weights, score each option, and watch the rankings update live.

Weighted scoring Each criterion has its own importance weight, so price does not have to count the same as speed or ease of use.
Live rankings Totals, percentages, and the winning option update automatically as you change any score.
Good for messy choices Useful for software picks, hiring decisions, product comparisons, travel plans, or any situation where gut feel is noisy.

Matrix Builder

Weights and scores use a 1 to 10 scale by default. Higher scores are always treated as better.
