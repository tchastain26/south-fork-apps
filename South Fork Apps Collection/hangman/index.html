How this round works

1. Pick your lane Category decides the word bank. Easy gives you 8 lives and reveals the opening letter. Standard gives 6. Hard gives 5.
2. Guess cleanly Correct letters fill every matching slot. Wrong guesses burn a life and draw the next body part.
3. Keep the streak alive Wins build streaks. One loss drops the streak to zero, but your best streak stays saved.
