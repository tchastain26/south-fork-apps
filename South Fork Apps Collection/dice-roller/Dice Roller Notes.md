# Dice Roller Notes

Created: 2026.05.02
Status: Done

## What It Does

Roll d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, and d100 with modifiers and roll history. Free online dice roller for tabletop games.

## How to Use

Open the app in a browser. No account or install required — everything runs locally.
