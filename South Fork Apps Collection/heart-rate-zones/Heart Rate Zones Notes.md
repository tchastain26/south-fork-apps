# Heart Rate Zones Notes

Created: 2026.05.22
Status: Done

## What It Does

Calculates your 5 aerobic training zones from your max heart rate. Supports both the simple % of max HR method and the Karvonen (heart rate reserve) method when you supply a resting heart rate. Max HR can be age-predicted (220 − age) or entered manually.

## How to Use

1. Enter your age (auto-calculates max HR via 220 − age formula).
2. Optionally enter a custom max HR if you know it from a fitness test.
3. Optionally enter your resting HR and switch to Karvonen method for more personalized zones.
4. Zones update instantly with BPM ranges and color-coded bands.
