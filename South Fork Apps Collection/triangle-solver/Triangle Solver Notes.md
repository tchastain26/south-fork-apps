# Triangle Solver Notes

Created: 2026.05.24
Status: Done

## What It Does

Solves a triangle from any valid set of three known values with at least one side. Supports SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, and ambiguous SSA input, then reports the solved sides, angles, perimeter, area, and a live diagram.

## How to Use

Enter exactly three known values, leaving the unknown fields blank. Click Solve Triangle to calculate the missing values, or load one of the example presets to test a common triangle case instantly.
