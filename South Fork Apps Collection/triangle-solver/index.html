Geometry Tool

Solve almost any triangle from three known values.

Enter any three known values with at least one side. This solver handles SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, and the tricky SSA case where two valid triangles may exist.

Accepted input sets Three values total. At least one side is required. Angles must sum to less than 180° before solving.
What you get back All side lengths, all angles, perimeter, area, height to side c, and a live triangle sketch.
