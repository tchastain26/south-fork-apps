# Music Intervals Notes

Created: 2026.05.09
Status: Done

## What It Does

Complete reference table for all musical intervals from unison through major 13th. Shows name, abbreviation, quality (Perfect/Major/Minor/Diminished), half step count, example from C, and consonance classification. Includes a compound intervals section.

## How to Use

Scroll through the two tables. Responsive — columns collapse on mobile.
