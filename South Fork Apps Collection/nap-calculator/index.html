How this works: Sleep runs in roughly 90-minute cycles. Waking at the end of a cycle means you surface from light sleep, feeling alert. Waking mid-cycle pulls you out of deep or REM sleep, producing grogginess. These times are estimates (it takes ~15 minutes to fall asleep on average).
