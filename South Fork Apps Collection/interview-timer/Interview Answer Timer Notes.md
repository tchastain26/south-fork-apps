# Interview Answer Timer Notes

Created: 2026.05.22
Status: Done

## What It Does

Timed practice for interview answers. Presets for 30s, 60s, 90s, 2min, 3min. Ring progress indicator, color shifts from green to yellow to red at 50%/25% remaining. Audio beep at half-time and on completion. Optional question text display. Custom timer support.
