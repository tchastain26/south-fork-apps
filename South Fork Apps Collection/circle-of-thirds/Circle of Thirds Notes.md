# Circle of Thirds Notes

Created: 2026.05.09
Status: Done

## What It Does

Interactive SVG Circle of Thirds visualization. Arranges all 12 chromatic notes in a circle grouped by minor third relationships (rather than fifths). Click any note segment to highlight it and see its major third (+4 semitones) and minor third (+3 semitones) neighbors — both ascending and descending. Shows how thirds build triads (major third + minor third = major triad). Includes explanatory text on chromatic mediant chord relationships.

## How to Use

Click any note on the circle. Green highlights = major third neighbors, blue highlights = minor third neighbors. Info panel shows the specific note relationships.
