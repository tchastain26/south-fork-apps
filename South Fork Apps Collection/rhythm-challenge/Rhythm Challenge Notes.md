# Rhythm Challenge Notes

Created: 2026.05.09
Status: Done

## What It Does

Rhythm tapping game. Generates random 16-step rhythm patterns from a library of 10 patterns across 3 difficulties. Plays the pattern back via Web Audio click track, then asks the user to tap it back. Scores based on how many expected beats were matched and average timing error in milliseconds. Tracks best score across rounds. BPM and difficulty are adjustable.

## How to Use

Click "New Pattern" to generate and hear a pattern. Press TAP (or Space/Enter) to tap back the rhythm. Score and timing error display after the round ends.
