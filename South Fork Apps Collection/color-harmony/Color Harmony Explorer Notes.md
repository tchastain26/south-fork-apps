# Color Harmony Explorer Notes

Created: 2026.05.22
Status: Done

## What It Does

Generates 9 color harmony sets from a base color: complementary, analogous (30° and 60°), triadic, split-complementary, tetradic square, double split-complementary, shades, and tints. Click any swatch to copy its HEX.
