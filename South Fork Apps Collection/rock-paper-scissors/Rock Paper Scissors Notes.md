# Rock Paper Scissors Notes

Created: 2026.05.24
Status: Done

## What It Does

Rock Paper Scissors lets you play quick rounds against the computer with saved scores, streak tracking, keyboard shortcuts, and a rolling history of your most recent matches.

## How to Use

Click Rock, Paper, or Scissors (or press R, P, or S) to play a round. Use Play Another Round to reset the prompt without clearing scores, or Reset Scores to wipe the scoreboard and history.
