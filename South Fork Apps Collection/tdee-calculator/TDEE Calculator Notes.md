# TDEE Calculator Notes

Created: 2026.05.22
Status: Done

## What It Does

Calculates Total Daily Energy Expenditure using the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR formula. Supports imperial and metric units. Shows BMR, TDEE, BMI, and a calorie table for common goals (extreme cut to moderate bulk).

## How to Use

Enter age, weight, height, sex, and activity level. Click Calculate. The goal table shows calorie targets for cutting, maintaining, or bulking.
