# Break-Even Calculator Notes

Created: 2026.05.23
Status: Done

## What It Does

Break-Even Calculator helps users estimate how many units they need to sell to cover fixed costs, how much revenue that requires, how much each sale contributes after variable cost, and how many units are needed to hit a profit target.

## How to Use

Enter fixed costs, variable cost per unit, selling price per unit, and an optional target profit. Review the break-even units, revenue, contribution margin ratio, and per-unit visual breakdown. Use the presets for quick sample scenarios.
