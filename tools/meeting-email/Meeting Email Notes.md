# Meeting Could've Been an Email Notes

Created: 2026.05.05
Status: Done

## What It Does

A 7-question quiz that scores a meeting on a 0-100 "email scale" and delivers a blunt verdict. Questions are weighted by how strongly each factor points toward a meeting being unnecessary. Five verdict tiers from "Shockingly Justified" to "Aggressively an Email." Includes animated progress dots, question slide transitions, count-up percentage animation, answer breakdown, and a shareable verdict string.

## How to Use

1. Answer each of the 7 yes/no questions about your meeting.
2. Get your email score and verdict.
3. Copy the verdict text and send it to the person who called the meeting.
4. Hit "Test Another" to reset.
