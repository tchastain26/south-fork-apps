About this tool

      
Meeting Agenda Builder

      
Meeting Agenda Builder turns a list of topics into a timed agenda with owners and a running total, then copies it out as clean plain text or Markdown ready to paste into a calendar invite or a document.

Why timing each item changes the meeting

      
An untimed agenda is a wish list. Assigning minutes to each item forces the trade-off out into the open before the meeting rather than in the last five minutes of it, when the final item silently gets dropped.

      
Naming an owner per item does the same for responsibility. An item with no owner has no one preparing for it, which is the usual reason a topic gets deferred to the next meeting.

Common mistakes

      
    
        
  • Scheduling every minute. Leave slack, because discussion never lands exactly on the estimate.
    • 
        
  • Writing a goal that is really a topic. Align on priorities is a goal; Q3 planning is a subject heading.
    • 
        
  • Putting the most important item last, where it is the one that gets cut.
