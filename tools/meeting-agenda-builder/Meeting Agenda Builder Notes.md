# Meeting Agenda Builder Notes

Created: 2026.05.24
Status: Done

## What It Does

This app builds a timed meeting agenda with editable agenda items, owners, minutes, and notes. It automatically totals the meeting length, highlights the longest segment, and generates copy-ready plain-text or Markdown output for sharing or saving.

## How to Use

Edit the meeting title, context, and goal at the top, then add or remove agenda items as needed. Fill in each topic, owner, minutes, and notes field. Use the copy buttons to grab either a plain-text or Markdown version of the finished agenda.
