# Circle of Fifths Notes

Created: 2026.05.09
Status: Done

## What It Does

Interactive SVG Circle of Fifths showing all 12 major keys (outer ring) and their relative minors (inner ring). Click any segment to highlight it and see the key's signature, accidentals, relative minor, and all 7 diatonic chords with Roman numeral analysis.

## How to Use

Click any key segment on the circle. The panel on the right updates with that key's full details including its diatonic chord set.
