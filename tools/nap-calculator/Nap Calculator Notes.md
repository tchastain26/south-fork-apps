# Nap Calculator Notes

Created: 2026.05.05
Status: Done

## What It Does

Calculates optimal nap or sleep times based on 90-minute sleep cycles. Three modes: "Nap Now" (given a start time, shows when to wake), "Wake Up At" (given a wake time, shows when to start the nap), and "Bedtime" (given a wake time, shows what time to go to sleep for 6, 7.5, or 9 hours). Each option has a quality dot indicator, a "Recommended" badge on the best option, and a midnight-crossing note when relevant. Auto-fills current time on load.

## How to Use

1. Pick a mode with the tab buttons.
2. Enter a time (or hit "Now" for the current time).
3. Review the three options and their quality ratings.
4. The recommended option is highlighted in green.

## Sleep Science

- Sleep cycles are approximately 90 minutes.
- Waking at the end of a cycle = light sleep = alert wake.
- Waking mid-cycle = deep or REM sleep = groggy wake.
- Power nap (20 min) avoids deep sleep entirely for a quick boost.
