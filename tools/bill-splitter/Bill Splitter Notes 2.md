# Bill Splitter Notes

Created: 2026.05.02
Status: Done

## What It Does

Split a restaurant bill unevenly between any number of people. Assign individual subtotals and split tax and tip proportionally. Free online bill splitter.

## How to Use

Open the app in a browser. No account or install required — everything runs locally.
