About this tool

Mobile Detailing Price Calculator

This is a live, working mobile detailing price calculator. A customer picks a vehicle type, a package, the condition and any add-ons, and gets an itemised estimate with a booking action, without having to call for a price.

How to use it

  1. Choose a vehicle type and a detailing package.
  2. Set the vehicle's condition and add any extras.
  3. The estimate updates immediately.
  4. Copy the quote, or use the booking action.

Why an instant estimate wins work

Most detailing sites make a price enquiry a phone call. That works for the customers who ring, and loses the ones who wanted a number at eleven at night and moved on to the next result. An instant range answers the question at the moment it is being asked.

The estimate is deliberately a range with a note that a final price follows an in-person inspection. That protects the business from being held to a number sight-unseen while still giving the customer something concrete enough to act on.

Worked example

How the estimate is built up:

  • Base packageby vehicle type
  • Conditionlight, average or heavy
  • Add-onspet hair, stain extraction, engine bay, headlights, ceramic, odour

Result: An itemised range rather than a single number, so the customer sees what drives the price.

When it helps

  • Giving customers a price at the moment they ask, including outside business hours.
  • Qualifying enquiries, since people who see the range and still book are ready to pay it.
  • Cutting time spent quoting jobs that were never going to convert.
  • Trying the calculation before deciding whether you want a branded version for your own business.

Common mistakes

  • Publishing a single fixed price sight-unseen. A range with an inspection caveat protects you from a job that turns out to be far worse than described.
  • Setting the add-on prices below what the work actually costs you in time.
  • Hiding pricing entirely, which loses every customer who wanted a number before calling.

What this tool handles

  • Estimates run entirely in the browser. This demo does not collect, save or transmit any customer information.
  • A version branded with your own business name, colours, packages and prices is available as a one-time setup, delivered as a hosted page or an HTML file with no subscription. Details and pricing are on the page below the calculator.
