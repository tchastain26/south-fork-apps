# Statistics Calculator Notes

Created: 2026.05.24
Status: Done

## What It Does

Computes descriptive statistics for any pasted numeric dataset, including mean, median, mode, range, quartiles, variance, standard deviation, and a frequency table. Handles comma, space, tab, and line-break separated input directly in the browser.

## How to Use

Paste a list of numbers into the dataset field, choose whether you want sample or population variance, then click Analyze Dataset. Use the sample buttons for quick test data or copy the finished summary after the stats are generated.
