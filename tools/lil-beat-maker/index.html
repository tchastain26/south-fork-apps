About this tool

Lil Beat Maker

Lil Beat Maker is an eight-track step sequencer that runs in the browser using the Web Audio API. You switch steps on and off in a grid, set the tempo, and it loops. There is no signup, no download, and no sample pack to install.

How to use it

  1. Click cells in the grid to turn steps on. Each row is one drum sound and each column is one step in time.
  2. Choose 16 or 32 steps depending on whether you want a one-bar or two-bar loop.
  3. Drag the BPM slider anywhere from 60 to 200.
  4. Press Play to loop it, Randomize for a starting point, or Load Groove for a preset pattern.

How the grid maps to time

A 16-step grid is one bar of 4/4 counted in sixteenth notes. Columns 1, 5, 9, and 13 are the four downbeats, and everything between them is a subdivision.

Step length follows directly from the tempo. At 120 BPM a quarter note is half a second, so a sixteenth note is 125 milliseconds and the whole 16-step bar loops every 2 seconds. Halve the tempo and every step doubles in length.

Worked example

The classic four-on-the-floor house pattern on a 16-step grid at the default tempo:

  • Kicksteps 1, 5, 9, 13
  • Snare or clapsteps 5 and 13
  • Closed hatevery odd step
  • BPM120

Result: A two-second loop with a kick on every beat, a backbeat on 2 and 4, and eighth-note hats running underneath.

When it helps

  • Sketching a drum pattern before you commit to opening a full DAW.
  • Making a quick loop to practice an instrument over.
  • Teaching or learning how a beat is actually built, one subdivision at a time.
  • Testing whether an idea in your head works before you spend an hour producing it.

What this tool handles

  • Everything runs client-side through the Web Audio API, so nothing is uploaded and the page works offline once loaded.
  • The 32-step mode gives you two bars, which is enough room for a fill or a variation on the second half.
  • Tempo can be changed while the loop is playing.
