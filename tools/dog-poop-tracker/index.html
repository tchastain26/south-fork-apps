About this tool

Dog Poop Tracker

Dog Poop Tracker maps where your dog has gone in the yard using GPS, so cleanup is a route rather than a search. Markers can be added, corrected and cleared, and everything is stored on your device.

How to use it

  1. Start with a simple yard map, or use Yard setup to capture four GPS corners.
  2. Press Mark when your dog goes, and the position is recorded.
  3. Select a spot and tap Mark cleaned as you pick up. Undo reverses an accidental cleanup.
  4. Drag or nudge a marker if the fix was poor.

Why phone GPS needs the extra work

Phone GPS can be wrong by many feet, especially near buildings and trees. Even a steady reading cannot reliably identify a small object. Use the map as a reminder and confirm the spot by sight.

The tool filters recent readings and rejects outliers. The estimate keeps the device-reported accuracy as a floor. Shaded areas also include known corner error. These are search areas, not a promise that a small object is inside them. Hand placement is often more useful in a small yard.

When it helps

  • Finding everything in a large or overgrown yard.
  • Cleaning up after dark, when searching visually does not work.
  • Exporting a backup to transfer the yard to another device (there is no automatic sync).
  • Keeping track across a few days between cleanups.

Common mistakes

  • Treating a GPS estimate as an exact spot. Use Place on map whenever the position looks wrong.
  • Calibrating corners in a hurry. The yard transform depends on those four points and errors propagate to every marker.
  • Expecting pinpoint precision under heavy tree cover, where consumer GPS simply cannot deliver it.

What this tool handles

  • Location data stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the app needs location permission only while you are using it.
