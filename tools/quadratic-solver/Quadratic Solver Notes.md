# Quadratic Solver Notes

Created: 2026.05.24
Status: Done

## What It Does

This app solves quadratic equations in standard form and shows the full work, not just the roots. It handles distinct real roots, repeated roots, and complex roots, while also calculating the discriminant, axis of symmetry, vertex, y-intercept, and drawing a live parabola graph.

## How to Use

Enter values for `a`, `b`, and `c`, then click Solve Equation. Use the presets for quick examples. The result area shows the root type, exact numeric outputs, and the step-by-step math underneath.
