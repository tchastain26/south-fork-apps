# Wind Chill & Heat Index Notes

Created: 2026.05.22
Status: Done

## What It Does

Calculates wind chill (NWS formula, valid ≤50°F, ≥3mph) and heat index (Rothfusz regression, valid ≥80°F, ≥40% humidity). Supports °F/°C and mph/km/h. Shows danger warnings for extreme values.
