Pig Latin Translator

Translate English to Pig Latin. Preserves capitalization and punctuation.

Rules: Words starting with a vowel → add -way (e.g. apple → appleway). Words starting with consonants → move leading consonant cluster to end, add -ay (e.g. string → ingstray).
