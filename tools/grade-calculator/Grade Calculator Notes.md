# Grade Calculator Notes

Created: 2026.05.22
Status: Done

## What It Does

Weighted grade calculator. Add assignments or categories with a score and weight percentage. Shows weighted average, letter grade, and total weight assigned. Includes a final exam calculator showing the score needed to hit a target grade.

## How to Use

Add each assignment with its score and weight. The summary updates automatically. Use the final exam section to find what score you need on remaining work.
