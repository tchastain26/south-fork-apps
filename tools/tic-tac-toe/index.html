How to use it

1. Pick your setup Computer mode gives you a solo game. Two-player mode turns the board into a local pass-and-play match.
2. Make your move Click a square or use keys 1 to 9. Top row is 1 2 3, middle is 4 5 6, bottom is 7 8 9.
3. Run it back New Round keeps the scoreboard. Clear Scores wipes both scoreboards and starts fresh.

What is tracked

Computer mode Human wins, computer wins, and ties are stored separately from local two-player results.
Two-player mode X wins, O wins, and ties stay grouped under the shared board scoreboard.
AI behavior The computer evaluates every legal line before choosing. It will not intentionally blunder.
About this tool

Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe plays against an unbeatable computer opponent or a second player, with score tracking.

How to use it

  1. Choose single player or two player.
  2. Take turns placing your mark.
  3. Get three in a row to win.

A solved game

Tic tac toe is solved, meaning the outcome with perfect play from both sides is known: it is always a draw. The unbeatable opponent uses that. It will never lose, and it will win only if you make a mistake.

The strategy fits in a few rules. Take a win if available, block the opponent's win if not, then take the centre, then a corner, then an edge. The centre is the strongest opening square because it sits on four of the eight winning lines, while a corner sits on three and an edge only two.

When it helps

  • A quick game against a real opponent or the computer.
  • Teaching a child their first strategy game.
  • Demonstrating what a solved game means.
  • Learning why the centre square matters.

Common mistakes

  • Opening on an edge, which is the weakest first move.
  • Chasing your own three in a row while ignoring the opponent's.
  • Expecting to beat perfect play. A draw is the best result available.
