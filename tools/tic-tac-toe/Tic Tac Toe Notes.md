# Tic Tac Toe Notes

Created: 2026.05.24
Status: Done

## What It Does

Tic Tac Toe is a browser-based game with local two-player mode, an unbeatable computer mode powered by minimax, keyboard shortcuts, and separate scoreboards saved in local storage.

## How to Use

Choose whether you want to play against the computer or a second player, then click a square or use keys 1 through 9 to place moves. Use New Round to keep the scoreboard going, or Clear Scores to reset both scoreboards.
